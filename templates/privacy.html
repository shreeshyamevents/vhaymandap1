Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 September 2026

VyahMandap ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

1. Data We Collect

  • Identity data: Name, mobile number, email address, company name (vendors)
  • Booking data: Equipment rented, dates, venue address, payment UTR
  • KYC data (for bookings ≥ ₹30,000): Aadhaar number, PAN number
  • Payment proof: Screenshots uploaded for verification
  • Communication: In-app chat messages, support tickets
  • Technical data: IP address, device info, timestamps

2. Why We Collect It

  • To operate the equipment rental marketplace
  • To verify bookings and process payments
  • To comply with tax and KYC regulations
  • To resolve disputes and prevent fraud
  • To send booking notifications

3. How Long We Keep It

  • Account data: Until you delete your account
  • Financial records: 7-8 years (Income Tax Act requirement)
  • Equipment condition reports: 30 days (auto-deleted)
  • Chat messages: Until account deletion

4. Who We Share It With

  • Vendors — see customer name, masked mobile, venue
  • Customers — see vendor name, masked mobile
  • Service providers — Cloudinary (image hosting), Telegram (notifications)
  • Government authorities — when legally required

We do not sell your data to third parties.

5. Your Rights (DPDP Act)

  • Right to access — view all data we hold. Visit My Data.
  • Right to portability — download your data as JSON. Visit My Data.
  • Right to erasure — request account deletion with a 30-day grace period. Visit My Data.
  • Right to grievance redressal — contact our DPO (below).
